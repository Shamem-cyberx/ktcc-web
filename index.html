About KTCC W.L.L

Proudly Building Bahrain Since 2005

Kapisha Trenchless and Contracting Company WLL (KTCC) has been a cornerstone of Bahrain's construction sector, delivering landmark infrastructure projects that have shaped the nation's development. As a 100% Bahraini-owned company, we combine international expertise with deep local knowledge to deliver excellence in every project.

From the iconic structures of Manama to the expanding communities across the governorates, KTCC has played a vital role in Bahrain's Vision 2030 economic development plan through our commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainable construction practices.
