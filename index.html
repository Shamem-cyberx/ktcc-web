About KTCC W.L.L

Trenchless delivery and civil contracting for utilities, corridors, and authority-led projects across Bahrain.

KTCC W.L.L is a Bahrain-based trenchless and civil contractor focused on underground utilities and corridor work with minimal surface disruption. We plan and execute HDD and related crossings alongside conventional civil packages, aligned to local authority requirements.

From dense urban routes to open corridors, we support government and private clients with engineered trenchless crossings and integrated civil scope.
